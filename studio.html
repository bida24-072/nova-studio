About Us

The Studio

We are a collective of architects, designers, and thinkers driven by a shared belief in the transformative power of good design.

Our Story

Founded in 2012, Nova Studio began as a small practice with a radical idea: that architecture should not just house people, but elevate their everyday lives.

Today, we operate globally, but our philosophy remains the same. We work closely with our clients, treating every project as a unique collaboration. We don't impose a "style"; instead, we uncover the inherent potential of a site and its inhabitants.
